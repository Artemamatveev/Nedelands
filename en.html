Frequently asked questions

Is Bittertalen made by DUO?

No. Bittertalen is an independent practice app. You will find the real exams and the official practice exams at DUO and on inburgeren.nl.

Which exam do I need: A2 or B1?

That depends on your situation, for example which civic integration law applies to you. Check inburgeren.nl or ask your municipality. In Bittertalen you choose your level yourself, and you can change it later.

Does Bittertalen help with naturalisation too?

For naturalisation you usually need a civic integration diploma, at level A2 or higher. Bittertalen helps you practise for the exams and teaches you about the Netherlands. The IND decides on your application: check ind.nl for what applies to you.

What is KNM?

Knowledge of Dutch Society: questions about work, housing, health, public bodies, history and how people in the Netherlands treat each other. You practise the eight themes of the exam, together or one at a time.

Do I need an account?

No, you can start right away. With a free account you keep your progress on all your devices and get your writing and spoken answers checked more often.

Does it work offline?

Yes, most exercises also work offline, for example on the train. Checking by Bittertalen needs an internet connection, and on some phones so does speech recognition.

Can I put Bittertalen on my phone?

Yes. Open Bittertalen in your browser and add it to your home screen (on an iPhone in Safari: Share, then Add to Home Screen). It then opens like an app.

Does a good score mean I will pass?

No. Your score shows how many answers were right in practice. The real exam can be different, so also take DUO’s official practice exams.
